# Changelog

## 1.700 · 19 November 2026

- G and Z from the page of sentences (p6): smaller and calmer than the ones from the alphabet. Three shapes of G and two of Z; the letter after it picks one.
- Y from "Yara" (p6). The old one read almost as a slash.
- The rest of that page was tried in a version 1.6 and left out: it was written faster, and the letters made the font less even.
- First build from this repository: OTF, TTF and WOFF2 from one script, with designer, URL and license in the font.

## 1.500 · 19 November 2026

- er, et and t from words written in one go (p5): more upright, and a t with a short crossbar. Two shapes of er and et, two of t.
- First contextual alternates: the letter before picks the shape.

## 1.400 · 19 November 2026

- Even stroke weight: every glyph is grown or thinned to the same width of line, the way an Edding writes.
- Spacing per letter by its shape, and kerning after T, V, W, Y, F and P.
- oo, sch and the IJ moved to discretionary ligatures: "ook" read as "cok".
- u, t, G, Z, 2 and E made narrower. er 10% larger, so its e matches the loose e.

## 1.300 · 19 November 2026

- The family is called PDK Hand, the style Edding 141F.

## 1.200 · 19 November 2026

- Symbols for feedback (p3, p4): arrows, ✓ ✗, + = % × < >, €, $, # * [ ] _ … – ~, and □ as the bullet.
- The smiley ◡̈. Ç Ø Œ Æ. The other accented letters are made from Pieter's own letters and accents.
